ABOUT HANDS AGAINST CHILD MARRIAGE

A Youth-Led Movement Protecting Childhood

Hands Against Child Marriage is a youth-led grassroot organisation dedicated to protecting childhood and promoting the power of choice to end child marriage. Our approach is grounded in community-led action and amplifying survivor voices.

WHY CHOOSE US

Our Value & Competitive Advantage

Our integrated approach combines education, empowerment, and justice to address child marriage holistically. We build local capacity, tackle youth unemployment through social work training, and create sustainable solutions.

What makes your approach unique?

We focus on a holistic approach covering education, empowerment and justice, while building local capacity and leveraging social work practitioners to create sustainable change.

Is your model scalable and replicable?

Yes! Our integrated framework can be implemented across all regions of Uganda and adapted to other countries to address child marriage and childhood sexual violence effectively.

How do you ensure sustainability?

We focus on training and capacity building of children, parents, teachers, and community leaders, empowering them to support and advocate for themselves and reduce reliance on external assistance.

OUR LOCATION

Based in Bugiri District, Uganda

Located in the Eastern part of Uganda in Busoga Sub-Region, our offices are in Bugiri Municipal Council, Eastern Division, Nkusi B village. From here, we serve communities throughout the region.
